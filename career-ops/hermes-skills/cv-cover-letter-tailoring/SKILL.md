# Application packages

Use the native `career_cv_compile` and `career_cover_compile` tools. The retired span-editing and character-matching process is not the production path. Do not reload this skill or entire historical career-context bundles during a guarded application request.

Research the company and the specific role before writing. Use the supplied JD and current official company information; do not substitute generic company claims. Keep research bounded and reuse results already available in this request.

Use owner-confirmed facts supplied in current owner context and the compiler's canonical sources. `career_cv_master` exposes verified CV facts when needed. Never invent experience, metrics, dates, qualifications or tools. Follow the latest canonical owner policy for eligibility. Only an explicit mandatory security-clearance requirement rejects a role; other concerns are review flags, not permission to fabricate qualifications. Do not introduce visa or sponsorship discussion into application documents or owner-facing application replies.

1. Read the JD once. Establish company, role and meaningful short role words.
2. Call `career_cv_compile` with the full JD, job_id, company and short_role. Use one native call at a time. Do not import tool_describe or compilation tools into execute_code; do not batch local tools.
3. Unless the owner requested CV only, call `career_cover_compile` with company, role and five truthful paragraphs totalling at most 2,855 characters. Follow the owner's writing preferences. Include relevant evidence and specific motivation for this role.
4. Return the verified PDF paths from PASS results as attachments. Compilation, export and delivery are distinct. Do not say a file was delivered until the adapter confirms it. Do not ask the owner to locate a Windows folder on their phone.

The compiler writes substantially role-specific wording from verified facts, independently reviews factual support and JD relevance, and preserves the approved mirror layout. A generic default or cosmetic synonym changes must not pass as tailoring. Never relax its checks, change the template, use shell/Python PDF repair, substitute another application's document or attach a failed/stale PDF.

On a short continuation, resume the pending application package and reuse matching verified outputs. Build only the missing or changed part. A provider failure must not erase an already successful document. A cover correction is bounded by the tool's existing correction budget; repeating rejected text is not progress.

If a stage cannot finish, report the exact stage and reason and return any independently verified documents. Never describe reading or printing a JD as building. Do not recommend raising global iteration limits to bypass a failed workflow.
