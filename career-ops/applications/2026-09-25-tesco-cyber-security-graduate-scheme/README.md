The JD is unusually explicit that it does **not** require a technical background — it screens on
*potential, curiosity and a willingness to learn*, plus problem solving, risk spotting and
adaptability. It also includes a **Customer Service rotation**, which plays directly to the
owner's Co-op Person in Charge and hospitality experience.

security-platform or cyber-assurance work — which is precisely what a graduate scheme exists
to supply.
