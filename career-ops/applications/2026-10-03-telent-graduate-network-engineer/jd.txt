What you’ll be doing

 As a Graduate Engineer, you'll become an active member of our engineering teams from day one. You'll work on real projects, contribute to technical solutions, and learn from experienced engineers across a range of disciplines. Our two-year programme is founded on recognised industry standards and features six-month rotations across departments tailored to your interests. You’ll gain practical experience and industry knowledge, plus a mentorship to guide you toward professional development and IET accreditation.

Depending on your placement and specialism, your day-to-day activities may include:

Supporting the design, deployment and maintenance of cloud and network infrastructure.
Assisting with the implementation of new technologies and services.
Working with experienced engineers to troubleshoot and resolve technical issues.
Learning industry best practices for security, automation, governance and operational support.
Participating in project meetings and collaborating with teams across the business.
Contributing to technical documentation, designs and service improvements.
Exploring opportunities to automate routine tasks and improve efficiency using modern engineering tools.
Building relationships with colleagues, customers and technology partners while developing your professional network.
Gain exposure to cloud platforms, networking technologies, automation, security and operational best practices.
What you’ll bring

A minimum of a 2:2 Honours Degree in a relevant STEM subject (Computer Science, Systems Engineering, Networking, Electronics, Cyber Security, IT etc.) achieved within the last two years.
Strong organisational skills and attention to detail
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, combined with proven team working and leadership abilities
Self-motivation, able to work on your own initiative and under pressure
Practical use of Microsoft Office applications
