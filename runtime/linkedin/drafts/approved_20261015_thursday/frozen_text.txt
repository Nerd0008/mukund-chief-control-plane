I spent this week building something that runs while I am asleep.

It drafts, checks and schedules work on its own. When it works, that feels like leverage. When it fails, it fails quietly, which is the part I keep thinking about.

I would rather a system stopped and told me it was stuck than carried on and was wrong.

So most of the work went into the stopping. Checks that refuse to continue. Records that prove what was approved. One rule above all: if you cannot verify it, do not do it.

The value is not in what a system does. It is in what it refuses to do.

What is the last thing you built that you trusted to run unattended?
